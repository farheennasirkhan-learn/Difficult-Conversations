Separate the Person From the Problem

Hard conversations go wrong when the issue becomes an attack on a person.

Attack the problem. Stand alongside the person.

Against the person
Against the problem
"You didn't get us the credentials."
"The missing credentials are now blocking the milestone."
"Your spec was wrong."
"The spec and the production data don't match. Here's the gap."
"You keep changing your mind."
"The scope has shifted three times. Let's lock it so we can deliver."

Same facts. The right framing keeps the other person on your side of the table.
