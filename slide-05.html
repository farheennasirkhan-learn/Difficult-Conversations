CLEAR: Structure the Conversation

Five moves carry almost any hard conversation. The word is the reminder.

C
Center the goal Decide what must be true when this conversation ends.
L
Lead with the issue Name it plainly in one sentence. No burying it.
E
Empathize Acknowledge the other person's stake before defending yours.
A
Anchor in facts Use evidence and specifics, not blame or adjectives.
R
Resolve to a next step Leave with a decision, an action, or a clear path forward.

Start by naming the issue, not by softening it into nothing.
