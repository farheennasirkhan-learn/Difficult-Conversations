Common Mistakes in Hard Conversations

Common mistake
Better move
Burying the point in cushioning
Lead with the issue in the first three sentences.
Waiting until you have a full solution
Raise it early, with options instead of answers.
Making it about the person
Attack the problem; stand with the person.
Defending instead of listening
Acknowledge first, respond second.
Winning the argument, losing the relationship
Aim to stay trusted, not to be right.
Going silent after a decision you disagreed with
Disagree once, then commit.

A strong difficult conversation feels direct, fair, and finished.
