PAUSE: Stay Composed When It Heats Up

When a conversation turns emotional, your regulation sets the temperature for the room.

P
A
U
S
E

Pause. One breath before you respond.

Acknowledge the emotion, theirs and yours.

Understand the real concern under the heat.

Slow the pace. Lower your volume, not your clarity.

Engage the issue, never the person.

You cannot control whether the other person escalates. You can control whether you join them.
