Delivering Bad News

Bad news does the least damage when it is early, specific, and paired with a path forward.

A strong bad-news message does three things:

Step 1

Name it clearly

State what went wrong without hedging

Step 2

Own your part

Take responsibility where it's yours

Step 3

Offer the path

Come with options, not just a problem

How to say it

"The model retraining introduced a regression we didn't catch before the demo. That's on us. Here are two options: roll back to the stable version today, or hold the demo two days while we fix it properly. I'd recommend the rollback so you keep the date."
