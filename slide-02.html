Why Difficult Conversations Are Part of the Job

FDEs are embedded where the stakes are real and the news is not always good.

A timeline slips. A client's assumption is wrong. A request is out of scope. A stakeholder is blocking the work. Your own lead made a call you disagree with.

Avoiding these moments feels kind. It is not. The conversation only gets harder the longer it waits.

Clients and colleagues judge an FDE on two things at once:

The work

Did you deliver?

The way you handle the hard moments

Could they trust you when it got uncomfortable?

The question behind every difficult conversation

"Will this person be straight with me, even when it's hard?"
